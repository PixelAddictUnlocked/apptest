Scoring Design
Principle: Every question gets 3 options, scored 1, 2, 3. Each question has a polarity — whether a higher value means better or worse wellbeing. The final wellbeing score is always 0–100% where 100% = best wellbeing.

Here's the polarity analysis for every question:

Burnout (all negative — higher = worse)
"I feel mentally exhausted" → Never(1)=good, Sometimes(3)=bad → negative
All 8 burnout questions use agreement_5 and are negative statements → negative
Mental Wellbeing (all positive — higher = better)
"I've been feeling optimistic" → Never(1)=bad, Sometimes(3)=good → positive
All 5 mental questions are positive statements → positive

Sleep (mixed)
"I've been going to sleep same time" → agreement, positive
"I sleep 7-9hrs" → agreement, positive
"I am awake for less than 30mins..." → agreement, positive
"I stay awake all day without dozing" → agreement, positive
"How long to fall asleep?" → time_duration, negative (longer = worse)
"How many nights do you awaken?" → nights_per_week, negative (more = worse)
"How long are you awake for?" → time_duration, negative (longer = worse)
"Rate your sleep quality?" → rating, positive
"I am satisfied with my sleep" → agreement, positive

Lifestyle (mixed)
"Physical activity frequency" → frequency, positive (more = better)
"Rate nutrition" → rating, positive
"Stayed hydrated" → agreement, positive
"Alcohol consumption" → frequency, negative (more = worse)
"Nights away from home" → nights_away, negative (more = worse)
"Travel impact on wellbeing" → slider, negative (higher impact = worse)
"Workload manageable" → rating, positive
"Connected to family/friends" → rating, positive
"Time for hobbies" → rating, positive
"Financial pressures" → slider, negative (more pressure = worse)
"Overall wellbeing" → rating, positive
